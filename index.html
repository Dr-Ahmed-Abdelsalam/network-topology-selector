          Method & sources

          How the numbers are made

          Three layers: fixed baseline scores, weights from your priorities, and explicit scenario checks.

          Weighted Sum Model

          S(t) = Σ w(c) · s(t,c) ⁄ Σ w(c)

          • s(t,c): baseline score of topology t on criterion c, 1–10. Fixed and identical for every user.
          • w(c): weight 0–5. In Decision mode each weight starts at 3 and moves by the documented amount for each answer; you can then adjust it.
          • Scenario checks never change scores. They mark options as not recommended (legacy technology, impractical size, missing redundancy) and add hybrid designs.
          • Sensitivity: each weight is moved ±1, and 500 seeded random variations of ±25 % test how often the recommendation holds.
